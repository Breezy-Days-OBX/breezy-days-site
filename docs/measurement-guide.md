# Measurement Guide

Measure observed public-site behavior for 60–90 days after launch. The range is a logging window, not a performance promise or a claim that a particular traffic volume will be reached.

## What to log

Use a client-owned, access-controlled reporting location. At a consistent review interval, record the observation date, source, device context when available, and counts for:

- availability-request submissions recorded in Netlify Forms;
- qualified requests, quotes sent, agreements sent, payments received, and completed reservations only when the owner has an authoritative operational record;
- public analytics events limited to the approved fixed categories: primary request click, form start, form validation error, form submit, form success, and approved marketplace outbound click;
- operational exceptions such as Form delivery issues, owner-settings recovery, or a production smoke-test failure.

Do not put names, emails, phone numbers, dates, messages, payment data, Identity data, or dashboard activity into analytics or this aggregate log. Keep raw inquiry handling in the approved client process, not in the website repository.

## Review method

1. Establish the first observed period as the baseline; do not infer a before value that was never measured.
2. Compare like-for-like observed periods and record material site, traffic-source, seasonality, pricing, or process changes beside the counts.
3. State results as observed counts and documented context, not causal lift, revenue, or conversion claims unless the underlying record supports that conclusion.
4. At the end of the 60–90 day window, decide whether the observations justify a scoped follow-up review. Preserve the raw source location and method used for every reported number.

## Not included

Ongoing inbox coverage, guest screening, calendar synchronization, quote delivery, agreement administration, payment collection, tax handling, marketplace management, ad buying, SEO services, analytics administration, incident monitoring, legal review, and platform-account management are not included in this coded website deliverable. They require a separately approved owner or service-provider process.
